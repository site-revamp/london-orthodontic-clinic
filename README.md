# London Orthodontic Clinic Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

The London Orthodontic Clinic is a private orthodontic practice in Central London, specialising in Invisalign, clear aligners and other teeth-straightening treatments. It also lists general dental services, including check-ups, hygiene, fillings and composite bonding.

The redesigned homepage positions the clinic as a modern Invisalign Experience Studio, bringing its orthodontic experience, digital treatment technology, flexible payment options and patient-focused care into a clearer presentation. It is aimed at adults, teenagers and families considering orthodontic treatment.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign focuses on three key areas:

1. **Clearer Invisalign Positioning**  
   The homepage gives greater prominence to the clinic's Invisalign Experience Studio identity, treatment experience and Apex Provider status. This makes its primary specialisation clearer to visitors.

2. **Stronger Treatment and Technology Presentation**  
   Orthodontic treatments and digital capabilities, including iTero scanning, 3D smile visualisation and Virtual Care monitoring, are presented as part of the clinic's treatment offering. This helps visitors understand the available treatment approaches and technology.

3. **More Accessible Appointment and Payment Information**  
   Consultation booking, flexible payment arrangements, finance options and Central London accessibility are incorporated into the homepage direction. These details help visitors assess practical considerations when choosing the clinic.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** Prioritises the Invisalign Experience Studio positioning and key treatment information.
- **Content organisation:** Separates orthodontic treatments, general dental services and digital technology for easier navigation.
- **Conversion focus:** Gives greater attention to consultation booking and treatment enquiries.
- **Patient experience:** Makes payment flexibility, multilingual care and accessibility easier to discover.
- **Brand presentation:** Supports a modern, digitally advanced clinical identity while keeping the focus on orthodontic expertise.

## Project Objective

Create a focused homepage that communicates the clinic's Invisalign specialisation, explains its treatment and technology offering, and makes it easier for prospective patients to understand their options and enquire about a consultation.
